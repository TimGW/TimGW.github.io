Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by TimGw on Google Play and other app stores ("the apps"). These are personal, non-commercial hobby projects. Their source code is publicly available on GitHub.

Information collected

The apps do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising. Unless stated otherwise inside an app, the apps do not collect or store personal information on any server operated by me.

Data you enter in an app (such as settings, timers or to-do items) is stored locally on your device. Some apps offer features that require an account or online synchronisation (for example a shared to-do list). In that case the data needed for that feature, such as your account identifier, display name and the content you create, is stored using Google Firebase.

Permissions

Some apps request device permissions, such as location or motion sensors, only to provide their core functionality (for example a compass that points towards a location). This data is processed on your device and is not shared with me.

Third-party services

The apps may use the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, crash reporting and analytics)

These services may collect anonymous technical information, such as device model, operating system version and crash logs, to help keep the apps stable.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the app. If you created an account in one of the apps and want your data deleted, you can request this using the contact details below.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your data, please contact me via GitHub or LinkedIn.
